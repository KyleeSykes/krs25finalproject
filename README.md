I am creating a fantasy world, and within this fantasy world, there is a magical character who lives in a cottage right outside of the town
practicing witchcraft and other forms of sorcery unbeknownst to the town members. The town people live their mundane, routine lives not 
knowing the immense level of magic that is being practiced on the hill above them. While the Halloween festival begins in the town, the 
player, who is the sorcer/sorceress, emerges and explores the town safe from being caught. I am inspired by witchcraft themed media where 
the character is hiding due to their knowledge of magic. The characters' experience will be one where they attempt to explore the boundaries
of their magic powers throughout the town and wilderness that surrounds it. I 3D sculpted a model of a jack-o-lantern to demonstrate my "something
more" and also explore my Halloween theme more. I am proud of my development of my own creation/model into my Unity game. I have never attempted
this before, and it felt satisfying to see my creation go from an app on my IPad to an interactable object in my VR project.
